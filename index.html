Our story

A little shop in Paldi with a very big oven.

The Cake Boutique started with one idea: a celebration cake should taste as thoughtful as it looks. So we keep the menu tight, the recipes honest, and the finishing touches by hand.

Birthdays, weddings, a Tuesday that deserves dessert. Whatever the reason, we bake it with love.
