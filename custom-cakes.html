How ordering works

Four steps from idea to cake.

Please order at least 48 hours ahead. Tiered and sculpted cakes need 5 days.

  1. 01

    Share your idea

    Fill in the form below, or send us a reference photo on WhatsApp.

  2. 02

    We confirm the details

    We reply within a few hours with a design sketch and a final quote.

  3. 03

    Pay the advance

    A 50% advance locks your date. The balance is due on delivery or pickup.

  4. 04

    Baked fresh, delivered safe

    Baked the morning of your event, boxed carefully and delivered across Ahmedabad.
